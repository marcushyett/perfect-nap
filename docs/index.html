Common questions

Why is the predicted nap time different from what my book/app says?

Perfect Nap personalises predictions to your baby. Each completed nap of at least 25 minutes nudges a per-baby multiplier (between 0.75× and 1.25× of the clinical baseline) so that, over time, predictions converge on what actually works for you. The Settings screen shows the current factor.

Does the app work without internet?

Yes — Perfect Nap makes no network calls. Everything runs on device and data stays on device. You don't need an account.

I deleted the app. Did my data go with it?

Yes. Data is stored only on the device. There is no cloud backup. We may add iCloud sync in a future version; until then, deleting the app removes all logged sleep.

The Live Activity isn't showing on the Lock Screen.

Live Activities require iOS 16.1 or newer, and they need to be enabled in Settings → Perfect Nap → Allow Live Activities. They also appear only while a nap is currently running or a prediction is active.

Can I edit a nap I logged by mistake?

Yes. Open the History tab and tap any session to adjust start time, end time, or whether it was a nap or night sleep. The personalised learning will refresh.

About the science

Predictions combine an age-based wake-window baseline (Paruthi et al. 2016, Iglowstein et al. 2003, Weissbluth, Mindell & Owens) with a position factor (first/middle/pre-bed), a nap-quality factor, and a learned per-baby multiplier. The formal basis is the Borbély two-process model. Babies are individuals — the prediction is a starting point, not a prescription.
