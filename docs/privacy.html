Privacy Policy

Your baby's sleep data stays in your own iCloud. The only thing that reaches our server is a device notification token — never sleep data. This page explains it in plain English.

The short version

  • Your baby's sleep data lives on your device and syncs through your own iCloud account (Apple-encrypted). We can't read it.
  • iCloud sync lets you back up your data and share a baby with a partner, so you both see and log the same naps.
  • To update a partner's lock-screen timer the instant you start or stop a nap, the app sends a device notification token to our small relay server. No sleep, nap, or health data is sent to or stored on our server.
  • No analytics, no advertising SDKs, no third-party trackers. No account, no email, no login.

What the app stores (your device + your iCloud)

To do its job, Perfect Nap records:

  • Your baby's date of birth (entered during onboarding).
  • The start time, end time, and type (nap or night) of each sleep you log.
  • A per-baby personalisation factor learned from completed naps, and optional settings (bedtime, a trip).

This is held in Apple's Core Data and synced via iCloud (CloudKit) to your private iCloud account — for backup and for sharing a baby with a partner. It is stored in your iCloud, encrypted by Apple; we have no access to it and it is never sent to our own servers. It's also shared with the Perfect Nap widget on the same device via an App Group.

Our notification relay — what it does and doesn't store

To make a partner's lock-screen Live Activity update instantly (even when their app is closed), Apple requires a server to send the push. Ours is deliberately minimal. It stores only:

  • Apple push notification tokens for your device(s);
  • a random per-install identifier and the random identifier of the shared baby, used only to route the push to the right device.

It does not store your baby's sleep history, names tied to your identity, your email, your Apple ID, your location, or any analytics. Tokens auto-expire. These identifiers are not linked to your identity and are never used to track you.

What the app does not collect

Perfect Nap does not collect your name, email address, contact list, location, advertising identifier, photos, microphone or camera data, or any usage analytics. We do not sell or share any data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Children's data

A baby's date of birth and sleep times are stored only in the parent's own iCloud account and on their devices — never on our servers. Only device notification tokens reach our relay, and they are not children's data and are not used for tracking. The parent or guardian controls all of it.

Data we declare on the App Store

In App Store privacy terms we collect Identifiers (a Device ID — the notification token), used only for App Functionality (delivering notifications), not linked to your identity, and not used for tracking.
