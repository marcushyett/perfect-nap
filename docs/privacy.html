Privacy Policy

Perfect Nap collects nothing about you. This page explains, in plain English, what that means.

The short version

  • Perfect Nap stores everything you enter only on your device.
  • The app makes no network requests. There is no server, no account, no login.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or third-party trackers of any kind.
  • If you delete the app, your data is deleted with it.

What the app stores on your device

To do its job, Perfect Nap records the following on your device only:

  • Your baby's date of birth (entered during onboarding).
  • The start time, end time, and type (nap or night) of each sleep you log.
  • A per-baby personalisation factor that is learned from completed naps.

This data is held in Apple's local storage (SwiftData) and shared with the Perfect Nap widget extension via an App Group container on the same device. It never leaves the device.

What the app does not collect

Perfect Nap does not collect, transmit, or store any of the following: your name, email address, contact list, location, device identifier, advertising identifier, photos, microphone or camera data, or any usage analytics.

Notifications and Live Activities

If you grant notification permission, Perfect Nap schedules local notifications and Live Activities through Apple's standard system APIs. These are generated locally on your device. No notification content is sent through our servers (we don't operate any).

Children's data

Perfect Nap records a baby's date of birth and sleep times. Because this information is only ever stored on the parent's device and is never transmitted, no children's data is collected by us. The parent or guardian controls all data on their device.

If the app changes

If a future version of Perfect Nap ever adds a feature that requires sending data off the device (for example, iCloud sync), this policy will be updated before the feature ships, and the new behaviour will be described explicitly in the App Store release notes.
